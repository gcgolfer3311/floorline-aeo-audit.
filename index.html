    Questions

    What is an AI visibility audit?

    It checks whether AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can reach your website, understand who you are, and recommend you when customers ask for a business like yours.

    Is the audit really free?

    Yes. The technical scan is free with no signup. The live AI answer check is free too — it asks for your email so we can send the results and limit abuse.

    What is AEO?

    AEO (answer engine optimization) is making your business easy for AI answer engines to find, read, trust and cite — the AI-era version of SEO.

    Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

    No one can guarantee that. AI answers vary from run to run. What we can do is remove every technical barrier, make your business information clear and consistent, and track how often you're mentioned over time.
